Questions

Common questions

Does my small business really need a website?

Not always on day one. But if customers search for you on Google, compare you with competitors, or want to see your full products and prices, a website builds trust that a Facebook page alone can't. It's also the one place online that you own, so it doesn't depend on an algorithm.

How much does a business website cost?

My business website package starts from $600–$1,000, including up to 5 pages, mobile-friendly design, SEO and AEO setup, contact form, Google Maps and 2–3 short video ads. Landing pages and e-commerce stores start from 500,000–2,000,000 MMK, depending on size and features. I'll give you a clear quote after a free call.

Can you build an e-commerce website?

Yes. I design and build online stores with product pages, cart and checkout, and payment gateway integration, starting from 500,000–2,000,000 MMK.

Can you automate repetitive tasks with AI?

Often, yes. Good examples are replying to common customer questions, saving enquiries to a spreadsheet, drafting posts and product descriptions, and sending weekly summaries. Not every task is worth automating, though, and I'll tell you honestly which ones are.

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) means structuring your website so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can understand your business and recommend it when people ask questions like "best bakery in Yangon". It works alongside normal Google SEO.

Do I need to know how to code or be technical?

No. Everything I build is designed for non-technical owners, and every project includes a walkthrough and training.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. I can redesign an existing site or improve its content, structure and SEO.

Can you teach me instead of doing everything for me?

Yes, that's a big part of what I do. Start with my free tutorials, join the early-access list for the free Starter Kit and the AI Video Course, or ask about guided sessions where I help while you build.

What information do you need to start?

Just a conversation about your business and goals. If we go ahead, I'll ask for your logo, photos, product or service details, and examples of websites or content you like. I'll send a simple checklist so you know exactly what to prepare.

Do you work with Myanmar businesses?

Yes, Myanmar business owners are who I work with most. I'm based in Thailand and work remotely, communicating in Burmese or English over Viber, Telegram, Messenger, WhatsApp or video call.

Which AI tools do you use?

Mainly Claude, along with tools for scheduling, video and design. I choose based on your needs and budget, not on what's trendy.

How long does a project take?

AI Skills take about 3–7 days, AI workflows 1–2 weeks, and websites 2–4 weeks.

Can I combine services?

Yes, and bundles get a discount. Ask during your free call.

How does payment work?

50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Course payments are made in full at enrollment.

Not sure where to start? That's normal.

Tell me about your business, and I'll tell you honestly what would help most, whether that's a website, an AI workflow, or just a free guide to get started.
